# ♿ KITJOY – Assistive Technology for Non-Motorized Wheelchairs

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-prototype-blue)
![Topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/focus-Accessibility%20%26%20Inclusion-orange)

The **KITJOY** project aims to provide mobility and independence for people with physical disabilities (PwD) through the automation of conventional wheelchairs. It is designed as a flexible and low-cost alternative to expensive commercial motorized chairs, bridging the gap between technological availability and social necessity.

### 🔍 The Problem
In Brazil alone, approximately 2 million people require a wheelchair for mobility, yet only 10% manage to obtain equipment provided by the public health system. Commercial motorized wheelchairs often have prohibitive costs, leaving many users dependent on external help for simple daily tasks, which affects their physical autonomy and social participation.

---

### ✨ Our Solution
The **KITJOY** is an automation kit designed to be installed on conventional, non-motorized wheelchairs. By applying this kit, the chair becomes motorized, allowing the user to navigate independently using a **joystick**. 

- **Accessibility:** Low-cost components to ensure affordability.
- **Flexibility:** Designed to be adaptable to different wheelchair models.
- **Autonomy:** Focuses on empowering users with neuromuscular diseases or spinal cord injuries to move without constant assistance.

---

### 🚀 Tech Stack & Core Components
The prototype was developed using an intersection of hardware and software logic:

- **Microcontroller:** Arduino platforms for logic control.
- **Actuators:** High-torque motors for wheel propulsion.
- **Input:** Analog Joystick for directional and speed control.
- **Power:** Rechargeable battery systems for daily usage.
- **Structural Design:** 3D printed parts or customized mounts for easy installation.

---

### 🎯 Key Objectives
- **Motorize** conventional wheelchairs with an accessible automation kit.
- **Promote** autonomy for individuals with severe motor limitations.
- **Investigate** the impact of assistive tools on the quality of life and social inclusion of PwDs.
